# Privacy Policy for Zpěvníkátor

**Last Updated: May 9, 2026**

Petr Kratochvíla ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Zpěvníkátor** mobile application (the "App").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information or data that can identify you. The App is designed to function as a local tool for viewing and managing songbooks.

### Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data
While using our App, we do not ask you to provide us with any personally identifiable information.

#### Usage Data & Local Storage
The App may store certain information locally on your device to enhance your user experience. This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third parties. This includes:
*   **App Settings**: Such as transposition preferences and theme settings.
*   **Favorites**: IDs of songs you have marked as "liked" or "favorites".
*   **Local Database**: The song library is stored in a local SQLite database on your device.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App may load certain resources from third-party services. These services may collect technical information (such as your IP address or browser version) when you request these resources:
*   **Google Fonts**: We use Google Fonts to provide a consistent visual experience. When you use the App, your device may connect to Google's servers to download these fonts.

We do not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 3. Permissions

The App does not require or request any sensitive device permissions (such as Camera, Location, Contacts, or Microphone).

## 4. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Since all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on the security of your device itself.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
